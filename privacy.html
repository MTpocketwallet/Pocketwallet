Pocket Wallet privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Pocket Wallet is a personal savings-goal tracker made by an individual developer. It is provided as is, without warranty. Contact: mtpocketwallet@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device

Your goals, savings entries, notes, and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use. The developer cannot see data that stays only on your device.

Google sign-in and cloud sync (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, Pocket Wallet saves a copy of your goals, entries and notes to a database run by the developer, using Google Firebase. The copy is linked to your Google account so it can follow you to other devices. The developer can access this database. Google stores and processes the data under its own terms. Pocket Wallet doesn't add extra encryption beyond what Firebase provides. You can sign out in the app's settings at any time, which stops syncing, and you can ask the developer to delete your data by emailing the address below.

Exchange rates

To show currency conversions, the app requests public exchange-rate data from third-party services. These requests contain no personal information. The services may log standard connection details, such as your IP address, under their own policies. You can turn off these downloads with Airplane mode in settings.

Bill scanning

The bill scanner runs entirely in your browser. The text-recognition library is downloaded from a public CDN when you first use it. Photos are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

What we don't do

Pocket Wallet has no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no accounts of its own. We do not sell or share your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
